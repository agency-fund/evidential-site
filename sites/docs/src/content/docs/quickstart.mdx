## Log in

Once your organization is authorized, go to **[app.evidential.dev](https://app.evidential.dev)** and sign in with your organization's single sign-on provider.

## 5-Minute Setup

<video src="https://media.evidential.dev/docs/evidential_demo_20250709.mp4" poster="/media/evidential-demo-poster.webp" preload="none" controls width="100%" title="5-minute onboarding video"></video>

<Steps>

1. **Connect your data warehouse**

   We currently support BigQuery, Redshift, Postgres and Lakehouse. (If you'd like to run an
   API-only bandit, skip this step and specify the **API Only** data source.)

1. **Define experiment participants**

   For warehouse-backed experiments, point us to the table or view that lists your participants with the relevant
   metrics.

1. **Create an experiment**

   Name it, choose a randomization unit and traffic split, and pick metrics & filters. See
   [Experiment design types](/concepts/experiment-design-types/) to decide which design fits.

1. **Retrieve assignments**

   Download a CSV from the UI to use in your own service or fetch assignments via the
   [Experiments API](/guides/integrate-with-the-api/) (see the [API reference](/api/)).

1. **Analyze results**

   Track progress and analyze results in the built-in dashboard. (Depending on experiment type, your
   service may push outcomes to Evidential via the API; or we pull outcomes from your data warehouse
   for you.)

</Steps>
