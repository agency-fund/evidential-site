## Log in

Once your organization is authorized, go to **[app.evidential.dev](https://app.evidential.dev)** and sign in with your organization's single sign-on provider.

## 5-minutes Setup

<video src="https://media.evidential.dev/docs/evidential_demo_20250709.mp4" poster="/media/evidential-demo-poster.webp" preload="none" controls width="100%" title="5-minute onboarding video"></video>

<Steps>

1. **Connect your data warehouse**

   We currently support BigQuery, Redshift or Postgres. More connections may be coming.

1. **Define experiment participants**

   Point us to the table or view that lists your participants with the relevant metrics.

1. **Create an experiment**

   Name it, pick metrics & filters, choose randomization unit and traffic split.

1. **Retrieve assignments**

   Download a CSV from the UI or call the `/v1/assignments` REST endpoint.

1. **Analyze results**

   Use the built-in dashboard or query the experiment results endpoint.

</Steps>
